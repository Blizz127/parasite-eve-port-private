* For the next build's notes (fold into r6 / whatever ships branches
  claude/enh-move-speed and claude/enh-assists). Plain words, r5 style.
* New — optional, all OFF unless you turn them on (dev menu: Back/View +
  Start/Menu, or F8 with PE_DEV_MENU=1). Your choices are remembered between
  runs (~/.config/parasite-eve-port/enhancements.conf); delete that file to
  go back to the real game's behaviour.
  - Options > Enhancements > Movement Speed: Aya walks and runs 125–200 %
    faster in the field. Only Aya's own walking and running changes; battles,
    enemies, cutscenes, scripted walks and timers are exactly as in the real
    game.
  - Options > Enhancements > Always Run: Aya runs without holding Circle.
  - Assists > 1-Hit Kills: every shot or Parasite Energy hit Aya lands kills
    the enemy. Experience, BP and item drops are awarded as normal. Bosses
    that change form part-way through a fight have not all been checked with
    this on; if a boss fight seems stuck, turn it off for that fight (you can
    open the dev menu mid-battle).
  - Assists > God Mode (No Damage): Aya's HP never goes down from enemy hits
    or status effects. Story events are unchanged. (This is different from
    the older F2 cheat, which just refills HP every frame.)
  - Assists > EXP/BP Multiplier: x2 or x3 experience and Bonus Points from
    every battle, so levelling and BP spending go faster with no other change.
* Thanks to u/stellarola on Reddit for the idea of Aya's run/walk speed
  option, and to u/SandmansSlave on Reddit for the idea of the no-grind
  assists (x2/x3 EXP, God Mode).
